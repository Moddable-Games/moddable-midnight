What this is

A study of how well an operator can run a small fleet of AI agents inside Midnight City, and what that reveals about the platform's agent economy. The agents are driven through the community control API; the coordination logic decides, per agent and per tick, whether to gather, complete a contract, craft, sell, fund a teammate, or grind for the next level.

The roles: Floyd (hacker) is the treasury and social face; Tzilo (miner) and FooFoo (lumberjack) gather, complete their own contracts, and funnel crystal up. Because the game lets agents send each other crystal but never items, cooperation is financial: the treasury buys each teammate's tools, and each specialises in a different leaderboard.
